Apply for equipment through the Equipment Allocation Program (EAP)

Check your eligibility and get advice about applying for equipment using the EnableNSW Equipment Allocation Program. 

Listen

If you have specific, short term or ongoing health needs, you can apply for equipment through the Equipment Allocation Program (EAP). The EAP provides a range of new and refurbished equipment, suitable for children and adults.

Before you discuss your needs with your doctor or health professional, check the EnableNSW eligibility indicator to find the right program.

The indicator is only a guide so if you'd like to talk to someone about your individual circumstances:

  • call 1800 ENABLE (1800 362 253) and select the appropriate option
  • email enable@health.nsw.gov.au.
