# Medical aids and equipment

## Subsidies for aids and equipment at home

Assistance is available for eligible Queensland residents to access medical aids and equipment to use as home. These are run by the Medical Aids Subsidy Scheme (MASS). You need be assessed by a clinician to apply.

## Types of aids and equipment

A range of medical aids and equipment are subsidised through the Medical Aids Subsidy Scheme for eligible Queenslanders, helping them to live at home.

## Aids provided through hospital programs

MASS, medical aids subsidy scheme, eligible, forms, application guidelines, apply, cystic fibrosis program, assistance, oxygen, nebuliser, PEP device, flutter device, equipment, CFP, outpatient, assistive technology

## Looking after your aids and equipment

The Medical Aids Subsidy Scheme can help with the cost of necessary repairs for certain items subsided through the scheme. Items supplied through CAEATI or VOSS are not covered.
