# Other forms you may need to complete

When you make your claim for Carer Payment, we may ask you to give us more information.

You may need to complete some extra forms. Your claim will tell you which ones.

Your situation | Forms you may need to complete

- Carer Payment – Income and assets details - person 16 years or over

- Carer Payment and Carer Allowance – Medical Report for a person 16 years or over

- Carer Payment and Carer Allowance review of care provided – Caring for a person 16 years or over

You’re caring for someone 16 or older.

- Carer Payment – Income and assets details – child under 16 years

- Carer Payment and Carer Allowance – Care needs assessment (for a child under 16 years)

- Medical Certificate for Carer Payment for a child under 16 years

- Carer Payment and Carer Allowance – Medical Report for a child under 16 years

You’re caring for someone younger than 16.

You need to tell us about your finances. | Income and Asset information

You want someone else to deal with us for you. | Authorising a person or organisation to enquire or act on your behalf
