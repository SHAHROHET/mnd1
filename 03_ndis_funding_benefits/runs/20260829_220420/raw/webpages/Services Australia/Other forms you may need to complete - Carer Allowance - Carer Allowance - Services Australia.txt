# Other forms you may need to complete

When you make your claim for Carer Allowance, we may ask you to give us more information.

You may need to complete some extra forms. Your claim will tell you which ones.

Your situation | Forms you may need to complete

- Carer Allowance Questionnaire – Carer not living with the person for whom care is being provided

- Carer Payment and/or Carer Allowance – Medical Report for a person 16 years or over

- Carer Payment and Carer Allowance review of care provided – Caring for a person 16 years or over

16 or over

- Carer Payment and/or Carer Allowance – Care needs assessment (for a child under 16 years)

- Carer Payment and Carer Allowance – Medical Report for a child under 16 years

Under 16
