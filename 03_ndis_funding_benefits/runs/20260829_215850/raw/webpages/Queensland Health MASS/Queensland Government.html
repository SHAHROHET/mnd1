Can I apply?

MASS administers some specialty programs and schemes which may vary in eligibility requirements, please refer to guidelines.

  1. You will need to arrange a clinical assessment by a health professional (clinician) approved by MASS to prescribe the particular type of aid or equipment.
  2. The prescriber will assist you with the completion of application forms and/or submit the application on your behalf.

MASS administers some specialty programs and schemes which may vary in eligibility requirements.

Medical Aids Subsidy Scheme (MASS) eligibility includes:

  • Communication aids
  • Continence aids
  • Daily living aids and mobility aids
  • Home oxygen
  • Medical grade footwear
  • Orthoses

You may be eligible for assistance under MASS if:

  1. You are a permanent resident of Queensland.
  2. You have a permanent or stable condition or disability.
  3. You hold one of the following eligibility cards in your own name:
    • Centrelink Pensioner Concession Card
    • Centrelink Health Care Card
    • Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) Pensioner Concession Card (conditions apply)
    • Queensland Government Seniors Card
  4. You meet the clinical criteria for each aid.

Guidelines are available here:

  • Communication Aids (PDF 298 kB)

  • Daily Living and Mobility Aids (PDF 492 kB)

You may be eligible for assistance under Cystic Fibrosis Program (CFP) if:

  1. You are a permanent resident of Queensland.
  2. You hold one of the following eligibility cards in your own name:
    • Centrelink Pensioner Concession Card
    • Centrelink Health Care Card
    • Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) Pensioner Concession Card (conditions apply)
    • Queensland Government Seniors Card
  3. You meet the clinical criteria.

CFP Aids and equipment are not provided by the program for hospital inpatients and high
care residents of Commonwealth funded care facilities.

Cystic Fibrosis Program Guidelines
(PDF 298 kB)

You may be eligible for assistance under Queensland Heat Moisture Exchangers (HME) Subsidy Scheme for Laryngectomy (QHSSL) if:

  1. You are a permanent resident of Queensland.
  2. You hold a current Medicare Card OR an appropriate visa
  3. You have undergone laryngectomy
  4. You have completed an appropriate trial of laryngectomy respiratory consumables for a minimum of four (4) weeks OR providing evidence you are self-funding laryngectomy respiratory consumables.

Heat Moisture Exchangers via the Queensland HME Subsidy Scheme for Laryngectomy (QHSSL) Guidelines (PDF 310 kB)

Palliative Care Programs:

  • Palliative Care Equipment Program (PCEP) includes:
    • Continence Aids
    • Daily living and mobility aids
    • Home oxygen
  • Palliative Care Syringe Driver Program

You may be eligible for assistance under PCEP/PCSDP if:

  1. You are a permanent resident of Queensland with a Queensland delivery address
  2. Provide a Medicare Card number for purpose of unique identification. If you do not have one, please contact MASS.
  3. Have a Palliative Confirmation form completed
  4. Meet the clinical criteria for each aid.

You may be eligible under Queensland Artificial Limb Service (QALS) for definitive prosthetic services if:

  • Be aged 65 years or older;
  • be an Australian Citizen, Permanent Resident or a holder of a protected special category visa (SCV);
  • be listed on a current and open resident (Green) Medicare Card
  • have completed an ‘interim rehabilitation program’ at an amputee clinic, been assessed and deemed competent and suitable to use a prosthetic limb.
  • Not be obtaining prosthetic funding support or services through another Government agency or state service.

You are required to complete client registration form (PDF 869 kB) (PDF 869 kB) to register as a QALS client.

Queensland Artificial Limb Service Guidelines (PDF 422 kB)

You may be eligible for assistance under the Spectacle Supply Scheme (SSS) if:

  1. You are a permanent resident of Queensland.
  2. You hold one of the following eligibility cards for a minimum of six (6) months in your own name:
    • Centrelink Pensioner Concession Card
    • Centrelink Health Care Card
    • Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) Pensioner Concession Card (conditions apply)
    • Queensland Government Seniors Card
  3. You mhave a clinical need for spectacles as determined by an
    Optometrist/Ophthalmologist.
