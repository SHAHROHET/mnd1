# Paid Parental Leave scheme for employers

Support for employers to help their employees access Parental Leave Pay under the Paid Parental Leave scheme.

The Paid Parental Leave scheme includes Parental Leave Pay. It’s a payment that helps your employees, who are eligible working parents take time off to care for a newborn or recently adopted child.

If you’re an employer looking for more information, we have resources for the scheme including the Employer Toolkit available to help you.

Watch our video about the Paid Parental Leave scheme for employers.

Transcript | Watch on YouTube

This information about Paid Parental Leave is for employers. If you’re an employee, you can find out more about Parental Leave Pay .

How the scheme works

If you’re an employer, we’ll pay you under the Paid Parental Leave scheme so you can support your employee while they care for a newborn or recently adopted child.

How to register

Information on how to register your business for the Paid Parental Leave scheme.

How to respond to an Employer Determination

An Employer Determination is when we decide you need to provide an employee’s Parental Leave Pay under the Paid Parental Leave scheme.

How to manage your role in the scheme

As an employer, you have ongoing requirements to manage your role in the Paid Parental Leave scheme.
