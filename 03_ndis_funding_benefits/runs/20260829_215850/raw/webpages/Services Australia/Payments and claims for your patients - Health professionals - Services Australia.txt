# Payments and claims

Check MBS and PBS item numbers. Ways to bill and claim using ECLIPSE, Medicare Easyclaim, Medicare Online or HPOS. Check claim status. Manage rejected claims.

MBS claims

Check Medicare Benefits Schedule (MBS) item numbers. Submit MBS claims and Department of Veterans’ Affairs (DVA) claims. Access reports for MBS claims.

PBS claims

Check Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS) co-payments, pricing and item numbers. Submit claims and find updates.

MBS rejected claims

Troubleshoot rejected Medicare claims or Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) claims.
