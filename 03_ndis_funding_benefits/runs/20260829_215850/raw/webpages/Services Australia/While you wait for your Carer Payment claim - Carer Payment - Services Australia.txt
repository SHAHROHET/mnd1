# While you wait

There are some things you can do while you wait to hear about your Carer Payment claim.

## on this page

- Checking where your claim is up to

- Accessing other help

Carer situations are complex and it will take some time to assess your situation.

We’ll contact you if we need any more information to support your claim so we can fully understand your situation and the support you need.

We’ll never ask you for money to release your information or fix your account. Read more about How to protect against scams .

## Checking where your claim is up to

To check where your claim is up to you can use:

- your Centrelink online account using your myGov

- the Express Plus Centrelink mobile app.

You can also use your online account to:

- check messages from us

- update your details and tell us of any changes

- withdraw your application.

## Accessing other help

You can submit a claim for other payments while you wait for your Carer Payment claim.

We have a range of income support payments that you can claim while you’re caring for someone such as:

- JobSeeker Payment if you’re 22 or older

- Youth Allowance for job seekers if you’re 16 to 21

- Parenting payment if you’re caring for children.

We also have supports if you’re in crisis or need special help .

There are other organisations who can help you and the person you care for. Read more about services in your state and getting support .
