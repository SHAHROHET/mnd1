### Delivering person-centred support and specialist care to people affected by MND in Western Australia.

Members, clients and their carers are our prime focus. We strive to understand and meet their needs to enhance the quality of life care for people living with MND.

## We care for carers

##### We provide family carer information and support

Family and friends play an important role in caring for their loved one with Motor Neurone Disease (MND). Whilst the focus is often on the person with MND, we recognise that you might need extra support and information to help you through this time.

Support Services Carer Information

## We rely on your generosity & efforts

MNDAWA relies heavily on the generosity and efforts of the community to assist us in raising much needed funds and awareness for our specialised care and support services for families living with Motor Neurone Disease.
