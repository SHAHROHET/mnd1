# Education / Support (Gather)

#### Education / Support (Gather)

###### Gather by MNDAWA is a trusted place to connect with community, find support and access education for Western Australians impacted by MND.

Ga ther is a program of events which provide practical guidance, support and education designed for people living with MND, their carers and family members.

Our purpose is simple: to meet you where you are with support that is clear, practical and timely.

Whether you’ve just received a diagnosis, are adjusting to changing roles as a carer, or navigating the many changes that accompany MND, you don’t have to face it alone.

##### Let’s face this together.

###### Gather by MNDAWA incorporates the following programs:

Monthly Gather 🔵 🟠

A range of activities that foster social connection, encourage self-care and benefits physical, mental and emotional wellbeing.

Newly Diagnosed Gather 🔵 🟠

A safe and supportive space for people who have recently been diagnosed with MND, and their carers.

Carers Gather 🔵

A gently guided, supportive space where carers can learn more about MND, access practical tools to care for themselves and their loved ones, and connect with others.

Anticipatory Grief Gather (ONLINE) 🔵

Designed for carers to understand and acknowledge anticipatory grief, carer fatigue, pre-grief and death literacy and to support self-awareness of your own needs.

Bereavement Gather (ONLINE) 🔵

A welcoming, supportive space for recently bereaved carers and family members to learn about the grief process, share their experiences, and reduce feelings of isolation.
