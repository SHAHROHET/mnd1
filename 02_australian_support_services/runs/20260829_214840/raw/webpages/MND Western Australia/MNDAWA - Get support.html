The Motor Neurone Disease Association of WA is committed to providing specialised care and support services for people living with MND, their carers and families in Western Australia. 

MNDAWA provides care coordination and emotional support through our MND Advisory Service, connecting those living with MND to the services that they require. Some other supports we offer are:

Coordination of Support (NDIS)

MND Advisors offer invaluable expert guidance and emotional support to those diagnosed with MND and their families.

Read more
Occupational Therapy

An Occupational Therapist (OT) is an Allied Health Professional who will work with individuals to help them to maximize their independence and engage in the activities that are meaningful to them.

Read more
Respiratory Physiotherapy

All physiotherapy involves moving as best possible to maintain health and wellbeing.

Read more
Equipment

The Motor Neurone Disease Association of WA (MNDAWA) has an equipment pool which is available to people living with Motor Neurone Disease (MND).

Read more
Education and Support Groups

The Motor Neurone Disease Association of WA organises a range of different support groups and events throughout the year.

Read more


We understand that MND is unique because it progresses differently in every individual and recognise the importance for a timely response to the rapidly changing needs of people living with MND.


How to access our services

To access the services of MNDAWA please call the office on 6186 4133 or email us at admin@mndawa.asn.au and one of our team members will get back to you shortly.
