#### MND Queensland is a leading organisation wholly focussed on supporting people living with Motor Neurone Disease and their families, friends, and carers.

We offer understanding and practical assistance by delivering information, education and specialist support services.

## Access our care and support services

Our services support you at every step, from a free helpline and access to specialist health advisors, all the way to help navigating the NDIS to ensure you receive MND-appropriate care.

Call our free helpline service 1800 777 175
