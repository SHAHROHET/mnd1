MND Upskill

Living and working with MND

This unique workshop brings together people living with MND and their carers alongside health and community care professionals for a shared learning experience. This session is suitable for health and community care professionals from all disciplines, including allied health professionals, care coordinators and paid support workers. 

In this workshop, you’ll learn about what MND is, symptoms and symptom management strategies, explore communication aids and practice using some of the equipment available for hire from FlexEquip in a supportive and inclusive environment. This platform provides a great networking opportunity, and you’ll gain access to a range of resources to support ongoing learning and practical care.
