# Education and Support Groups

#### Education and Support Groups

###### MNDAWA organises regular education and information sessions for people living with motor neurone disease, family members, carers and friends.

MNDAWA organises regular education and information sessions for people living with motor neurone disease, family members, carers and friends. They provide information on a variety of topics relating to MND and participants are encouraged to ask questions.

MNDAWA’s support groups and events enable people living with MND and their families to feel connected, guided and supported through their journey with MND.

###### You, Me and MND Program

Our You, Me and MND Programs encompass our education, information, and support programs.

Read more

###### Support Groups

The Motor Neurone Disease Association of WA organises a range of different support groups and events throughout the year.

###### Ask the Experts

Ask the Experts is a one-day forum about motor neurone disease for people living with the disease, their families and friends.

### Upcoming support group events

Don't miss our MNDAWA support and education events in many different metropolitan and rural areas in Western Australia.
