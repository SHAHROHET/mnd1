Support and Services

MND NSW provides information, support and education about motor neurone disease for people living with motor neurone disease, their families, friends and carers. 

If you have been diagnosed with MND and would like to access support and services, please complete our Get Support: Intake Form online.

Info Line

The MND Info Line is for people from New South Wales, Australian Capital Territory and Northern Territory who want to know more about motor neurone disease.

Read more

MND Advisor

MND Advisors are qualified health and community care professionals with specific knowledge and experience related to MND.

Read more

NDIS Coordination of Support

Our Coordination of Support Service can assist you with the implementation of your National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) plan.

Read more

Carer Support

Caring for a loved one with MND means taking on new responsibilities. The experience can be both rewarding and challenging. 

Read more

Youth Hub

The Youth Hub has resources about MND for young carers. A young carer is a person between 5 and 24 years of age who has a family member or friend living with MND.

Read more

Support Groups

Attend your local MND Support Group to share your experience and be supported by others living with Motor Neurone Disease in your area.

Read more

Government support for you

In this section you will find information about the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) and My Aged Care (MAC). 

Read more

MND Genies

MND Genies is a closed Facebook group for people who have tested positive for an MND related genetic mutation, but do not have MND.

Read more
