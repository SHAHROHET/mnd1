## We will listen without judgement

Lifeline provides confidential crisis support that is accessible 24 hours a day.

- We encourage any person in Australia who is contemplating suicide, experiencing emotional distress, or caring for someone in crisis to call, text or chat with Lifeline.

- Regardless of your age, gender, ethnicity, sexual orientation, or religion, our trained crisis supporters are ready to listen, support, and help you work through what’s on your mind.

- We will...

- We will listen without judgement

- We will explore what’s troubling you

- Identify other forms of help, if that’s right for you

- We won't...

- We won’t pass judgement

- We won’t tell you what to do

- We won’t give advice

## Call us

### 13 11 14

Free and confidential help for anyone in Australia who is feeling overwhelmed, in emotional distress, or thinking about suicide.

When you call, you’ll be connected with a trained Lifeline Crisis Supporter who will listen with compassion, explore what you’re going through, and help you find a way forward.

Find out more Call now

## Text us

### 0477 13 11 14

If you prefer, you can connect with a trained Lifeline Crisis Support anytime via text message. Following a few optional pre-survey questions, who will listen, support you without judgement,

Find out more Text us now

## Chat with us online

Confidential one-to-one chat with a trained Lifeline Crisis Supporter.

If you are receiving a message wrongly implying you are outside Australia, please disable your browser ad blocker, or you can call or text us instead.

Chat now

## 13 Yarn

If you, or someone you know, are feeling worried or no good, we encourage you to connect with 13YARN on 13 92 76 (24 hours/7 days) and talk with an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander Crisis Supporter.

## Natural disaster support line

13 HELP (13 43 57) is Lifeline’s dedicated support line for people affected by extreme weather.

Whether you’ve experienced a flood, fire, cyclone, or another crisis, trained crisis supporters are available to listen, provide emotional support, and connect you with practical help.

The service is free, confidential, and available 24/7 to anyone in Australia.

Call now
