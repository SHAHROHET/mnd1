# Planning your palliative care

It's important to think about what you want when it comes to preparing your end-of-life care. Find out how to plan your palliative care and how to find a a palliative care provider to suit your needs.

- Think about what you want

- It is important to think about what you want when planning your end-of-life care.

- Start the conversation

- Talking about end-of-life care is different for everyone. It is important to let your loved ones know what your wishes are. Find out how to start the conversation.

- Advance care planning

- Find out what advance care planning is, how to make one and what we are doing to support advance care planning.

- Advance care directive

- Find out what an advance care directive is and how to make one.

- Find a palliative care service provider

- There are different kinds of palliative care service providers. Find out what they are and how to find the best provider for you or your loved ones.
