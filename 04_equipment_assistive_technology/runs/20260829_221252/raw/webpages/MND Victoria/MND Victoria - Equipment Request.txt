##### An equipment request must be submitted by an appropriate Allied Health Professional.

All requests for equipment provision from MND Victoria must be submitted online via the Online Request Form below.

Upon submission of the form:

- You will receive an automated confirmation of submission via email. If you do not receive this email, which may take up to 30 mins, please contact the Equipment Service (ES) by email or telephone. Contact details are listed below.

- You will receive a second email from the ES within 3 working days to advise equipment request status.

Online Request Form

###### Should you require any assistance, please contact the Equipment Service by calling (03) 9830 2122 / freecall 1800 777 175 or emailing equipment@mnd.org.au .
