Neurologists with expertise in MND

A neurologist is a specialist physician who diagnoses and treats conditions of the brain, spinal cord and nerves.

If MND is suspected, you will need a referral from your GP to see a neurologist. Only a neurologist can diagnose MND.

The diagnosis of motor neurone disease (MND) can be difficult and lengthy, so it is important to access a neurologist who has developed expertise in assessing and managing people with the disease.

The list below of Australian-based neurologists, organised alphabetically under state and public/private sector, all have recognised expertise in MND.

However, the list is not exhaustive and only includes neurologists known to MND Australia. We welcome suggestions for new entries or notification if any contact details below have changed, by emailing us at [email protected]

This list should not be taken or understood to be a recommendation by MND Australia or the MND State Association network. This is a list of known providers with experience treating MND. We highly recommend individuals do their own due diligence in seeking healthcare providers and services that are right for them.
