Life changes after an MND diagnosis.

Learning to live with the disease is incredibly challenging. There is a lot to consider. It can be hard to take it all in, but we are here to support you on your journey.

In this section, you’ll find information about managing the symptoms of MND, adjusting your daily routine with the help of equipment and other supports, making decisions about your care, and planning ahead.

We hope the information and resources here will help prepare you so you can live as well as possible for as long as possible.
