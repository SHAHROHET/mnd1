# Beyond Blue Email

Free mental health information service.

## What is email support?

Beyond Blue Email can answer your questions about mental health and help you find services or resources.

### How does email support work?

Send us an email with:

- a description of you and your situation

- your questions about mental health information, services or resources.

A qualified counsellor will reply to you within 24 hours. You won’t be able to reply to the email we send you but we’ll give you other options if you want to start a conversation.
