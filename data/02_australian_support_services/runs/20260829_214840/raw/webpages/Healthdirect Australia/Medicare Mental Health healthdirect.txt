## About this service

### Access

#### No appointment needed

This service does not require an appointment. Consider calling ahead to check service availability and wait times.

### Billing

#### No fee

A free service where no payment or Medicare card is required.

### Additional information

The Medicare Mental Health Phone Service is available to anyone living in Australia looking for advice, assessment and referral into and between mental health services.
