# Emergency contacts

1 minute read

Add to read later Print

If you are in crisis, anxious or depressed and want to talk with someone.

## Emergency help

### 000 Emergency

Call Triple Zero ( 000 ) in an emergency or if someone is in danger.

24 hours a day, 7 days a week

Call 000

### 106 Text Emergency Relay Service

Teletypewriters (TTY) users can text one zero six ( 106 ) in an emergency.

Use 106 relay service

### National Relay Service (NRS)

If you are d/Deaf, hard of hearing, or have a speech/communication difficulty please contact us through your preferred NRS call channel.

Using NRS in an emergency

### Emergency respite

If you become sick or injured and can’t continue to care, Carer Gateway can help to organise emergency respite services to take over while you recover, if a suitable service is available in your local area.

Go to Emergency respite page

## Need to talk?

### Lifeline

You can phone Lifeline to speak to a Crisis Supporter on 13 11 14 , text 0477 131 114 or chat to Lifeline online.

Go to lifeline
