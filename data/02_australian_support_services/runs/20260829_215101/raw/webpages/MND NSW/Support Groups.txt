# Support Groups

Attend your local MND Support Group to share your experience and be supported by others living with Motor Neurone Disease in your area.

Facilitated by your areas MND Advisor, the groups are an informal get-together held online and in-person across NSW and the ACT. Occasionally a guest speaker is arranged.

If you are a family member or friend caring for a person living with MND join our online support group, Carer Kitchen Table.

For Support Group locations and upcoming events, visit the Support Group events page or contact the MND Infoline on 1800 777 175.
