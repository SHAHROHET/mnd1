# Discounts and subsidies in your state if you have disability

Your state or territory government can help with discounts and subsidies if you have disability.

## On this page

- Companion card

- Transport discounts

- Mobility parking

- Patient travel subsidy

- Other discounts or subsidies

## Companion card

The companion card is for people with disability who need a high level of care in the community. If you’re eligible for a companion card, your carer may get free entry into some venues.

Find out more on your state or territory website.

State or territory | Resource

ACT | Companion Card

NSW | Companion Card

NT | Companion Card

Qld | Companion Card

SA | Companion Card

Tas | Companion Card

Vic | Companion Card

WA | Companion Card

## Transport discounts

In your state or territory, you may get:

- cheaper tickets on public transport

- taxi vouchers or subsidies

- subsidies if you need transport for regular medical treatment.

ACT | People with disabilities | Taxi Subsidy Scheme

NSW | Disability assistance

NT | Help getting around for seniors and people with a disability

Qld | Transport concessions

SA | Getting around with a disability or mobility aid

Tas | Transport Services Concessions | Disability assistance

Vic | Accessible travel | Community transport providers

WA | Transwa Concession | Travel subsidies

## Mobility parking

If you have disability, you may be eligible for a parking permit. Find out more on your state or territory website.

ACT | Disability parking permits

NSW | Mobility parking permits

NT | Disability Parking Permits

Qld | Parking permits

SA | Disability parking permit

Tas | Disability Parking Permits

Vic | Accessible parking permits

WA | Apply for an ACROD parking permit

## Patient travel subsidy

If you need to travel a long distance for medical treatment, you may get a subsidy for travel and accommodation.

ACT | Travelling to and out of Canberra for treatment

NSW | Isolated Patients Travel and Accommodation Assistance Scheme

NT | Patient Assistance Travel Scheme

Qld | The Patient Travel Subsidy Scheme

SA | Patient Assistance Transport Scheme

Tas | Patient Travel Assistance Scheme

Vic | Victorian Patient Transport Assistance Scheme

WA | Patient Assisted Travel Scheme

## Other discounts or subsidies

If you have disability, your state or territory may help with your household bills. Find out what support you can get with bills .
