Discounts and subsidies in your state if you have disability

Your state or territory government can help with discounts and subsidies if you have disability.

On this page

  • Companion card
  • Transport discounts
  • Mobility parking
  • Patient travel subsidy
  • Other discounts or subsidies

Companion card

The companion card is for people with disability who need a high level of care in the community. If you’re eligible for a companion card, your carer may get free entry into some venues.

Find out more on your state or territory website.

Transport discounts

In your state or territory, you may get:

  • cheaper tickets on public transport
  • taxi vouchers or subsidies
  • subsidies if you need transport for regular medical treatment.

Patient travel subsidy

If you need to travel a long distance for medical treatment, you may get a subsidy for travel and accommodation.
