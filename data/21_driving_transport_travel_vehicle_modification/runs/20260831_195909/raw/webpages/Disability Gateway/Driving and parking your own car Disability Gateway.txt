# Driving and parking your own car

Most local councils offer community transport to their residents who have limited or no access to private transport and have difficulty accessing conventional public transport. Contact your local council to find out about their community transport service.

## Find out what is available in your state or territory

Some people might drive their own car.

- getting your driver’s licence

- parking your car when you have a disability

- having changes made to your car so it’s easier for you to drive.
