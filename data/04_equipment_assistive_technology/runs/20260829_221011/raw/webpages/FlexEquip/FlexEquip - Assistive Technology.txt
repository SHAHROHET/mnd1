## Who is FlexEquip?

FlexEquip is expert in the provision of assistive technology (aids and equipment) for people living with motor neurone disease or NDIS participants.

FlexEquip is a service of Motor Neurone Disease NSW (MND NSW) and a registered NDIS provider.

## Accessing FlexEquip services

### Who can get assistive technology?

wheelchair_pickup

Adults who live in NSW or the ACT diagnosed with motor neurone disease or NDIS participants may be eligible to loan or rent assistive technology (aids and equipment) through FlexEquip.

Find out more

### How to get assistive technology?

groups

Occupational Therapists, Speech Pathologists and Physiotherapists assess your requirements in your primary residence.

They select items using the Product Library and submit a referral using FlexEquip Referral Cart.

### What does it cost?

question_answer

The FlexEquip range includes assistive technology (aids and equipment) that are loaned or rented to you. The returned items are checked and cleaned according to a strict cleaning protocol by FlexEquip staff prior to subsequent loan or rental.
