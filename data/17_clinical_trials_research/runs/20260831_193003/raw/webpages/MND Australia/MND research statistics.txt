### Prevalence of MND in Australia

In 2025, a report commissioned by MND Australia estimated there were 2,752 Australians living with MND. This is equivalent to approximately 1 in every 10,030 people. To read more information about MND in Australia, please read the 2025 report on the human and economic toll of MND, Every Moment Matters .
