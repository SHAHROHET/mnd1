Costs of healthcare

How do I pay for healthcare?

The Australian Government helps to lower the cost of healthcare for most people. Find answers to common questions about how to pay for healthcare.

Allied health

Allied health practitioners are trained professionals who are not doctors, dentists or nurses. Learn more about what these professionals do here.

About ambulances in Australia

Ambulances provide emergency transport for sick or injured people. Learn more about when to call triple zero (000) and how much it will cost you.

Australia’s healthcare system

The Australian healthcare system can sometimes seem confusing. Learn more about the types of services available, and how you can access them.

Bulk billing for medical services

Bulk billing in Australia is when your doctor bills Medicare directly as full payment for their services and you have no out-of-pocket expenses.

Cost of dental care

Dental care can be expensive and you may need to pay for it yourself. Find out the different ways you can cover the cost of dental care.

Financial support during long-term illness

Learn about financial support for long-term illness, including payments, healthcare costs, medicines and help with travel and daily living.

Management of chronic conditions

Chronic conditions are long-lasting and need ongoing medical attention. Learn how to manage your chronic condition and the types of support available.

Maternity care in Australia

Explore pregnancy care options in Australia. Learn about antenatal care, birth settings, and costs to make the best choices for you and your baby.

Medicare Safety Nets

Medicare Safety Nets help support people who spend a lot on medical services outside of hospital. Find out if you are eligible and how to register.

Paying for diagnostic testing

Diagnostic tests, such as pathology tests and imaging scans, can be expensive. Learn more here about the costs and what Medicare covers.

Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS)

The Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS) provides many prescription medicines for a reduced price. Learn how to get the most from the PBS.

Private health insurance

Private health insurance can cover ambulance transport, hospital stays and extras, such as optical and dental. Learn more about health insurance here.

Public and private hospitals – an overview

Discover the differences between public and private hospitals in Australia, including costs, services, and how to decide which option is best for you.

The role of a GP

Your GP (general practitioner) is usually the first person to go to if you’re sick. GPs coordinate your healthcare - watch this video to learn more.

Travelling to your healthcare appointment

If you live in a rural area, travel can be time-consuming and costly. Get help to reduce travel costs and make the most of your medical appointment.

Understanding the costs of mental health services

Mental health care costs can vary depending on the service you use. Learn if you are eligible for government rebates to help with the cost.

What is a referral?

A referral is a written request from your doctor or health professional to get expert help with diagnosis or treatment of your health problem.

What is Medicare?

Medicare is a national insurance scheme that provides free or subsidised healthcare for Australians Learn more about how it works and who's eligible.
