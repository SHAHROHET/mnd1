## Condition group

### All chronic conditions prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- 2022 survey estimates indicated that around 61% of people in Australia (15.4 million) were living with a chronic condition, defined as being diagnosed with at least 1 of 72 select conditions

- Data source: ABS (2023) Microdata: National Health Survey 2022, AIHW analysis of detailed microdata.

- Notes:

- Data reported in this chart include Australians living with at least one of 72 selected long-term health conditions. See here for a list of the selected long-term health conditions.

See report for more information

### Multimorbidity prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- 2022 survey estimates indicated that around 38% of people in Australia (9.7 million) were living with multimorbidity, defined as being diagnosed with 2 or more of 72 select conditions

- Multimorbidity refers to living with 2 or more chronic conditions at the same time.

- Data reported in this chart include Australians living with 2 or more of 72 selected long-term health conditions. See here for a list of the selected long-term health conditions.

### All cancer 40-YEAR prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2021)

- Around 5,500 people per 100,000 population (5.5%) were living with cancer in Australia in 2021, having been diagnosed at some point in the previous 40 years

- Data source: AIHW Australian Cancer Database 2021

- 40-year prevalence refers to the number of people diagnosed within the 40 years leading up to 31 December of a given year and alive on this date.

### All chronic kidney disease (CKD) prevalence (crude rate), for ages 18+, (2022–24)

- One in seven (14.2%) adults had biomedical signs of chronic kidney disease in 2022–24

- Data source: ABS National Health Measures Survey.

- See report for more information

### All chronic musculoskeletal conditions prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- An estimated 7.3 million people in Australia (29%) were living with at least one chronic musculoskeletal condition in 2022, based on survey data.

- Data source: ABS (2023) National Health Survey 2022.

### All chronic respiratory conditions prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- In 2022, around 34% of people in Australia (8.5 million) were estimated to be living with a chronic respiratory condition

- Data source: ABS (2023) National Health Survey 2022, AIHW analysis of National Health Survey 2022

### All diabetes prevalence (crude rate), for ages 18+, (2022–24)

- In 2022–24, an estimated 1 in 15 (6.5%) Australians aged 18 and over were living with diagnosed diabetes

- Data source: ABS National Health Measures Survey

### All neurological conditions prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- An estimated 2.2 million (8.7%) Australians were living with long-term neurological conditions – almost 1 in 11 Australians

- Data source: ABS (2023) National Health Survey 2022, AIHW analysis of detailed microdata.

### Dementia prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2024)

- It was estimated by the AIHW that around 425,000 (1.6%) Australians were living with dementia in 2024. This is equivalent to 16 people with dementia per 1,000 Australians

- Data source: AIHW estimates were derived using prevalence rates from the 2015 World Alzheimer report and Withall et al. 2014, and the ABS 2024 estimated resident population.

### Heart, stroke and vascular disease (HSVD) prevalence (crude rate), for ages 18+, (2022–24)

- In 2022–24, an estimated 6.4% of Australians aged 18 and over were living with HSVD, based on self-reported data

## Chronic kidney disease

### Chronic kidney disease (CKD) - Stage 1 prevalence (crude rate), for ages 18+, (2022–24)

- One in twenty (5.1%) adults had biomedical signs of stage 1 CKD in 2022–24, a stage with usually no symptoms

### Chronic kidney disease (CKD) - Stage 2 prevalence (crude rate), for ages 18+, (2022–24)

- One in twenty-five (3.9%) adults had biomedical signs of stage 2 CKD in 2022–24, a stage with usually no symptoms

### Chronic kidney disease (CKD) - Stage 3a prevalence (crude rate), for ages 18+, (2022–24)

- One in twenty-five (3.8%) adults had biomedical signs of stage 3a CKD in 2022–24, a stage when a person may start to feel unwell

### Chronic kidney disease (CKD) - Stage 3b,4,5 prevalence (crude rate), for ages 18+, (2022–24)

- In 2022–24, 1.5% of adults had biomedical signs of CKD stages 3b, 4 and 5, which includes progressed CKD and kidney failure

### Kidney failure with replacement therapy (KRT) prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2024)

- One in a thousand Australians were living with kidney failure and receiving KRT in 2024

- Data source: Australian and New Zealand Dialysis and Transplant Registry.

### Osteoarthritis prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- An estimated 2.1 million people in Australia (8.3%) were living with osteoarthritis in 2022, based on survey data.

### Osteoporosis/osteopenia prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- 2022 survey estimates indicated that around 850k people in Australia (3.4%) were living with osteoporosis or osteopenia, although prevalence is likely higher as many cases are undiagnosed.

- Because osteoporosis has no overt symptoms, it is often not diagnosed until a fracture occurs. It is therefore difficult to determine the true prevalence of the condition and reported prevalence is likely to be an underestimation.

### Rheumatoid arthritis prevalence (crude rate), for all ages, (2022)

- 2022 survey estimates indicated that around 510k people in Australia (2.0%) were living with rheumatoid arthritis.
