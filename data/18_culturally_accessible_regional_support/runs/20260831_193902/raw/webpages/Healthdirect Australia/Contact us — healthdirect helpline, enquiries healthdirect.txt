## Health support

### Online Symptom Checker

Use the healthdirect Symptom Checker to assess your symptoms in about 4 minutes and see if you need medical attention.

### Speak to a registered nurse

Call healthdirect on 1800 022 222 (also known as 1800MEDICARE nationally and NURSE-ON-CALL in Victoria) about any health concern — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Need extra support calling this number?

If you are d/Deaf, hard of hearing or find it hard to speak, call the National Relay Service on 1800 555 727 for support.

If English is not your main language and you need an interpreter, call TIS National on 131 450 and ask to be transferred to the helpline.
