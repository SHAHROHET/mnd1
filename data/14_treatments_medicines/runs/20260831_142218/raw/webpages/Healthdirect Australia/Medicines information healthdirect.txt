#### Accidental overdose of medicine

Accidental overdose of medicine can be very dangerous, but it is preventable. Learn how to avoid overdosing, and what medicines need special care.

Read more about Accidental overdose of medicine.

#### ADHD medicines

There are different types of medicine that you can use to help you manage ADHD. Read more about the types, benefits and side effects.

Read more about ADHD medicines.

#### Angiotensin converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitors and angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs)

You might take an ACE inhibitor or ARBs for hypertension or heart failure. They work by relaxing your blood vessels.

Read more about Angiotensin converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitors and angiotensin receptor blockers (ARBs).

#### Antibiotics

Antibiotics are used to treat infections caused by bacteria. Learn more about how antibiotics work, how to take them and antibiotic resistance.

Read more about Antibiotics.

#### Antidepressants

Antidepressants are medicines that treat depression and other mental health conditions. Read about the types, side effects and how to take them safely...

Read more about Antidepressants.

#### Antihistamines

Antihistamine medicines help reduce allergy symptoms. You can learn more about how they can treat or prevent conditions like hay fever and asthma.

Read more about Antihistamines.

#### Anti-inflammatory medicines (NSAIDs)

Anti-inflammatory medicines (NSAIDs) are used to relieve pain and fever or reduce inflammation (redness and swelling). Find out about NSAIDs here.

Read more about Anti-inflammatory medicines (NSAIDs).

#### Antipsychotic medicines

Antipsychotic medicines can help ease the symptoms of psychosis. Read about why you may need an antipsychotic medicine and possible side effects.

Read more about Antipsychotic medicines.

#### Antiretroviral treatment for HIV

Antiretroviral medicines stop HIV from multiplying and protects your immune system from damage. Learn more about antiretroviral medicines here.

Read more about Antiretroviral treatment for HIV.

#### Antivirals for flu (influenza) and COVID-19

Antivirals for the flu and COVID-19 can help prevent and treat severe infections. Learn more about them and if you are eligible to receive them.

Read more about Antivirals for flu (influenza) and COVID-19.

#### Asthma medicines

Asthma medicines include relievers and preventers. Most asthma medicines come as inhalers or ‘puffers’. Find out about combination inhalers.

Read more about Asthma medicines.

#### Blood pressure medicines

Blood pressure medicines ('antihypertensives'), such as ACE inhibitors, calcium channel blockers and diuretics help manage high blood pressure.

Read more about Blood pressure medicines.

#### Buying medicines online

There can be advantages to buying medicines online, but there are some risks too. Find out more about buying medicines online safely.

Read more about Buying medicines online.

#### Chemotherapy

Chemotherapy is medicine that aims to stop or slow down the growth of cancer cells. Find out how 'chemo' treatment works and its side effects.

Read more about Chemotherapy.

#### Cold, flu and COVID-19 medicines

Different medicines can help relieve your cold, flu or COVID-19 symptoms. Learn how to safety take them and when to visit your doctor.

Read more about Cold, flu and COVID-19 medicines.

#### Cold, flu and COVID-19 medicines for children

Some medicines can help relieve your child’s cold, flu or COVID-19 symptoms. Learn what’s safe to give to your child.

Read more about Cold, flu and COVID-19 medicines for children.

#### Diabetes medication

Read about diabetes medicines including insulin and other medicines to control your blood sugar levels, how to take them and their side effects.

Read more about Diabetes medication.

#### Disease modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs) for arthritis

DMARDs are medicines used to types of arthritis caused by inflammation. Learn how these medicines work and about their side effects.

Read more about Disease modifying antirheumatic drugs (DMARDs) for arthritis.

#### Diuretic medicines

Find out what diuretic medicines ('fluid tablets') are used for, how they work, the most common diuretics, and how to manage side effects.

Read more about Diuretic medicines.

#### eScript (electronic prescription)

An eScript is a digital version of a prescription that your doctor can use to prescribe medicine. Your doctor will send it to you by SMS or email.

Read more about eScript (electronic prescription).

#### General anaesthetic

A general anaesthetic makes you unconscious so you do not feel pain, move or be aware during surgery. Risks include nausea, sore throat and tiredness.

Read more about General anaesthetic.

#### Home medicines review

It’s important to manage your medicines correctly. A free home medicines review (HMR) by a pharmacist can help make sure that your medicines are safe.

Read more about Home medicines review.

#### Menopausal hormone therapy (MHT)

Menopausal hormone therapy (MHT) used to be called HRT. It is used to treat the symptoms of menopause, such as hot flushes. MHT comes in a few forms.

Read more about Menopausal hormone therapy (MHT).

#### Ibuprofen

Ibuprofen treats fever and pain caused by inflammation. Learn more about how it works, how to take it and its possible side effects.

Read more about Ibuprofen.

#### Immunisation side effects

Immunisation side effects are unwanted effects that may happen after you get a vaccine. Learn how to treat mild adverse events after a vaccine.

Read more about Immunisation side effects.

#### Lithium

Lithium is a mood-stabilising medicine used to treat some mental illnesses, such as bipolar disorder. Learn about the risks and benefits of this mood-...

Read more about Lithium.

#### Local anaesthetic

A local anaesthetic temporarily numbs a part of your body. Here's what you should know about 'having a local' and questions to ask your doctor.

Read more about Local anaesthetic.

#### Medicine recalls

Medicines may be recalled for a range of reasons. Learn who regulates medicine recalls, what to do if a medicine is recalled and where to return it.

Read more about Medicine recalls.

#### Medicines and alcohol

You need to be careful if you drink alcohol when taking some medicines. This includes over-the-counter medicines and herbal remedies. Learn more here.

Read more about Medicines and alcohol.

#### Medicines and breastfeeding

While most medicines are safe to take while breastfeeding, some can affect your baby. Learn out more about medicines and breastfeeding here.

Read more about Medicines and breastfeeding.

#### Medicines during pregnancy

Learn more about which medicines are safe to take during pregnancy, including over-the-counter medicine, herbal supplements and vitamins.

Read more about Medicines during pregnancy.

#### Medicines and driving

Some medicines can affect the skills you need to drive safely. Read here to learn how to stay safe and lower your risks while driving.

Read more about Medicines and driving.

#### Medicines for babies and children

Get practical tips on giving your baby or child medicine, measuring doses correctly and what to do if your child have side effects.

Read more about Medicines for babies and children.

#### Medicines safety for older people

Older people are at increased risk of medicine-related problems. Learn how to take your medicines safely and what to ask your doctor.

Read more about Medicines safety for older people.

#### Medicines to treat nicotine dependence

Learn how over-the-counter and prescription medicines, along with professional quit smoking services, can help you quit smoking and vaping.

Read more about Medicines to treat nicotine dependence.

#### Opioid medicines

Opioids are a group of medicines prescribed by doctors to treat pain. Learn about side effects, withdrawal symptoms and what to do in an overdose.

Read more about Opioid medicines.

#### Out-of-date medicines

All medicines have an expiry date displayed on their packaging. Learn about why medicines expire, and how to dispose of old medicines safely.

Read more about Out-of-date medicines.

#### Over-the-counter medicines

Over-the-counter (OTC) medicines are medicines you can buy without needing a prescription. OT medicines can cause side effects and drug interactions.

Read more about Over-the-counter medicines.

#### Pain-relief medicines

Pain-relief medicines include over-the-counter and prescription medicines. Learn about their side effects as well as when and when not to use them.

Read more about Pain-relief medicines.

#### Paracetamol

You can take paracetamol for mild to moderate pain. Find out when to take paracetamol, its side effects and what to do if you take too much.

Read more about Paracetamol.

#### Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS)

The Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS) provides many prescription medicines for a reduced price. Learn how to get the most from the PBS.

Read more about Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS).

#### Questions to ask before taking a medicine

It's important to learn about your medicines before you take them. Here are some questions you can ask your doctor or pharmacist about medicines.

Read more about Questions to ask before taking a medicine.

#### Reading CMIs

Consumer Medicine Information (CMI) leaflets provide information about the medicine you are taking. Learn where to get CMI pages and how to use them.

Read more about Reading CMIs.

#### Reporting a problem with a medicine

You should tell your doctor or health professional about any problems with your medicine. It’s also helpful to report the problem to the TGA.

Read more about Reporting a problem with a medicine.

#### Scheduling of medicines and poisons

The scheduling of medicines is a national classification system that controls how medicines and poisons are made available to the public.

Read more about Scheduling of medicines and poisons.

#### Side effects of medicines

Side effects are any unwanted effects of a medicine. Learn more about different types of adverse reactions and what to do if you have them.

Read more about Side effects of medicines.

#### Statins

Statins are medicines used to lower cholesterol and are recommended for people at risk of cardiovascular disease. Here's how statins work.

Read more about Statins.

#### Stimulant drugs

Stimulants are a class of drugs that make you feel more alert or energetic. They include drugs such as caffeine, some medicines, ice and cocaine.

Read more about Stimulant drugs.

#### Sunscreen

Learn how sunscreen protects your skin from sun damage, when and how to use it effectively reapplying every 2 hours and when to seek medical advice.

Read more about Sunscreen.

#### What are generic medicines?

Generic medicines have the same active ingredient as brand-name medicines. Find out about generic medicines, active ingredients and how they work.

Read more about What are generic medicines?.

#### Glossary medical terms

This glossary explains common medical terms in plain English, to help you better understand health information.

Read more about Glossary medical terms.
