Urgent Medical Treatment

There are times when a person’s life is in danger and a decision about life-sustaining medical treatment must be made quickly. The factsheet in this section explains the law relating to urgent (emergency) medical treatment. You will also find case studies, mythbusters and other resources about the law in this area.
