# About palliative care

Palliative care helps people live as fully and as comfortably as possible with a life-limiting illness. Find out where you can receive palliative care, what it costs, how to get it, and what we are doing about palliative care.

- What is palliative care

- Palliative care is treatment, care and support for people living with a life-limiting illness.

- Where is palliative care provided?

- When possible, you and your family can decide where you want to receive palliative care. Find out where that can be.

- What does palliative care cost?

- Find out what the cost of your palliative care might be. If you are a carer, find out what payments you may be eligible for.

- How to get palliative care

- Find out who provides palliative care and how you can access it.

- What we're doing about palliative care

- We work to make sure you get the highest level of palliative care. Find out about our strategies, programs and research.
