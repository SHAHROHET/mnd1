# Blue card applications

## Apply for a blue card or exemption card

Learn how to apply for a blue card or exemption card, including how to prove your identity and access the online applicant portal.

## Pay for your blue card application

Find out when you will need to pay for your application, what the fees are and how to pay.

## Processing times for blue card applications

Find out how long your application for a blue card or exemption card should take to progress, and what might cause delays.

## Who is eligible to apply for or hold a blue card

Find out about serious and disqualifying offences and what happens if you are issued with a negative notice or your card is suspended or cancelled.

## Blue card risk assessments

Find out more about the risk assessment process that will happen if the blue card check returns assessable information, including charges or convictions.

## Substitute signatories for Blue Card Services forms

Learn how substitute signatories can make an application or sign a form on behalf of individuals who do not have legal capacity.
