# Industry assistance schemes

Supporting industry growth, investment opportunities and encouraging innovation.

## Access support to develop innovative location based solutions

SPUR is your gateway to a wide-range of location and government data via the Shared Location Information Platform (SLIP) and the Data WA open data portal.

## Apply for a grant to develop innovative location based solutions

SPURonWA grants are available to Western Australian start-ups, businesses and individuals working on innovative location-based products or services.

## Apply for WA Innovator of the Year Awards

The WA Innovator of the Year program has showcased innovative and entrepreneurial individuals, businesses and creative minds since 2006.

## Developing Western Australia's workforce

The Department of Training and Workforce Development works together with stakeholders across government, industry and the community to establish strategies and programs that build WA’s workforce to meet economic and community needs.

## Find opportunities for innovators and investors

Opportunities for investment and growth in Western Australia’s innovation community.

## Find out more about WA's defence capability

Western Australian industries have the capacity and expertise to deliver across the six capability streams identified by Defence.

## Find support services and programs for innovators

The New Industries website lists support services and programs to help get the innovation community up and running.

## Health and medical life sciences industry: Accelerator Program Access Grants application information

A guide on how to apply for the Accelerator Program Access Grants program.

## Health and medical life sciences industry: Entrepreneur in Residence Scheme application information

A guide on how to apply for the Entrepreneur in Residence (EIR) Scheme.

## Health and medical life sciences industry: Market Access Grant Scheme 2023

Health and medical life sciences industry: Market Access Grant Scheme

## Health and medical life sciences industry: Market Access Grant Scheme 2024

This round supports Western Australian health and medical life sciences companies in advancing their business development through national and international conferences and events in 2024.

## Health and medical life sciences industry: Market Access Grant Scheme 2025

This round supports Western Australian health and medical life sciences companies in advancing their business development through national and international conferences and events in 2025.

## Health and medical life sciences industry: Market Access Grant Scheme 2026

This round supports Western Australian health and medical life sciences companies in advancing their business development through national and international conferences and events in 2026.

## Lithium industry support program: financial assistance package for downstream processing

Temporary waiver of government fees to support downstream processing of lithium

## Lithium industry support program: financial assistance package for projects

Waiver of port charges and mining tenement fees for lithium miners in the ramp-up phase of their project

## Lithium Industry Support Program: Loan Facility

Temporary interest-free loans to help lithium miners sustain their operations

## Local capability fund: Peel - Regional Business Round

Helping businesses in the Peel region enhance their business capability and supply capacity.

## Local capability fund: Pilbara - Regional Business Round

Helping businesses in the Pilbara enhance their business capability and supply capacity.

## Lower Carbon Grants Program - Gorgon Fund

The Lower Carbon Grants Program - Gorgon Fund will support green energy and greentech projects in Western Australia in support of a sustainable low carbon future.

## State Government Incentives for employers of apprentices and trainees

Financial incentives and support are available to employers to help with the cost of taking on an apprentice or trainee.

## Western Australian Defence Industry Workforce Office

The WA Defence Industry Workforce Office receives input from WA’s defence industry to undertake workforce analysis and planning; lead in the formulation of defence industry workforce development plans, initiatives and strategies; and identify future workforce needs and skilling priorities.

## Western Australian Training Awards

The WA Training Awards recognise excellence in vocational education and training (VET) and the outstanding achievements of our VET students, apprentices and trainees, practitioners and training providers, organisations and employers.
