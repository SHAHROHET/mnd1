# Apply for WA Innovator of the Year Awards

The WA Innovator of the Year program has showcased innovative and entrepreneurial individuals, businesses and creative minds since 2006.

Last updated: 16 November 2020

Application forms and materials will be made available during the nomination period each year.

The application process is a 2-stage process.

## Stage 1 - Written application

The first stage in the application process involves a written application addressing the evaluation criteria on the application form.

Stage 1 evaluations are based on written applications only.

## Stage 2 - Pitch presentation to the judging panel

Following the submission of the written applications, an evaluation panel for each category will evaluate eligible applications against the criteria and develop a shortlist for each category.

The final evaluation panel will select 4 finalists from each of the 2 categories:

- Emerging Innovation.

- Growth.

The 8 finalists and the unsuccessful applicants will be notified by email.

Finalists will present a 15-minute pitch to the evaluation panel, followed by 10 minutes of question and answer time.

Following the pitch presentations, the winners will be decided and announced at the awards ceremony.

## Further information

Applications are currently closed. For further information contact the Department of Jobs, Tourism, Science and Innovation.
