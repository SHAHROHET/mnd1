# How much you can get

There are different rates of Carer Payment for single and partnered people.

## on this page

- Tax on your payment

- Carer Supplement

The Department of Social Services adjusts these rates every 20 March and 20 September.

Pension rates per fortnight | Single | Couple each | Couple combined | Couple each separated due to ill health

Maximum basic rate | $1,100.30 | $829.40 | $1,658.80 | $1,100.30

Maximum Pension Supplement | $86.50 | $65.20 | $130.40 | $86.50

Energy Supplement | $14.10 | $10.60 | $21.20 | $14.10

TOTAL | $1,200.90 | $905.20 | $1,810.40 | $1,200.90

Read about how your relationship changes can affect your payment rate.

## Tax on your payment

If you or the person you care for are Age Pension age or above, your Carer Payment is a taxable Centrelink payment. We don’t automatically deduct tax from your payment, but you can ask us to do this for you. This can help you avoid a debt at tax time.

Find out how to deduct tax from your payment .

## Carer Supplement

If you get Carer Payment for a period that includes 1 July, we’ll automatically pay you Carer Supplement each year. Find out more about Carer Supplement .

Paying tax on your payment

You may have to pay income tax on your taxable Centrelink payments.

Transitional rate of pension

The transitional rate started in 2009. It's for pensioners who would get a lower pension rate if we used the new income test.
