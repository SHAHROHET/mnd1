# Residence rules

To be eligible for Carer Allowance you must meet the residence rules.

On the day you claim, you must be all of the following:

- living in Australia

- physically in Australia

- an Australian resident .

The person you are caring for must be:

- an Australian resident.

To keep getting your payment, you must both be living in Australia as Australian residents.

## When you need to wait

If you've recently arrived as a resident in Australia, you may have to wait one year to get this payment. There are some exemptions to this. Read more about the newly arrived resident’s waiting period .

Residence descriptions

You usually need to be an Australian resident or hold a specific visa type to get a Centrelink payment or concession card.

Newly arrived resident's waiting period

If you’ve recently arrived as a resident in Australia, you may have to wait to get payments or concession cards.
