# Victorian Aids and Equipment Program guidelines

Guidelines for providers of Department of Health funded aids and equipment services.

These guidelines provide a framework for delivering the Victorian Aids and Equipment Program (VA&EP).

These guidelines are intended for:

- VA&EP service providers

- Assistive technology (AT) practitioners

- Department of Health program managers

- Clients and relevant stakeholders
